In October 2025 I bought a commercial trash can from the Oak Park Business district on Facebook Marketplace for $25. I then placed it in front of my house and have since been maintaining it for the neighborhood. It gets a lot of use and the amount of trash in the area has seamingly decreased.

The Oak Park Business district had so many for sale and if I knew what I had found I would have bought more and gave them to others to maintain. Soon after I saw the same trash cans being sold for over $100 on Marketplace. Someone had bought a bunch to resell.

Regardless trash cans like this do pop up from time to time for sale on Craigslist or Facebook Marketplace for a decent price. So if you live in a place that gets a lot of foot traffic and are interesting in maintaining one yourself I think it is worth it.

## Issues I ran into

### No lock

The trash can did not come with a key for the lock on it but it was unlocked. The main issue was that the door would swing open very easily. I ended up solving this with some cable ties holding the door shut. Originally I had 6 of them but this was overkill and just made changing the trash bag harder. So I pared it down to 3 and it works perfectly.

### No internal can

When I bought it it did not have a an internal trash can to hold the trash bag, it was just the outer metal part. I had an old metal trash can I was hoping would fit but it did not. So for the first few weeks I just tied a trash bag to the opening. This did not work well, often coming off and people would miss the opening and it would start filling up outside the bag.

I tried to find the actual can that goes to this but I could not find anywhere selling it without full trashcan. I then went to Home Depot looking for trashcans that would fit and found nothing. But on my way back home I stopped at the Western Feed and Pet Supply on 34th and they had metal trash cans out front. The trash cans did not have their measurements listed but it looked right so I took the chance and it fit perfectly.

### No rain guard

This trash can originally had a top part that went over the top that effectively blocked rain from getting into the can. I don't know if this part also has other uses or a name but it didn't have one of these with it. So when it rained the bag and internal can would fill up with water and become super heavy and gross. One time the bottom of the bag blew out because of this. Covering everything with trash water.

I solved this by drilling holes in the bottom of the internal can and adding a small hole to the bag in the rainy season. Since I have not had an issue with more than a little bit of water pooling in the bottom of the bag.

### The trash bag kept falling

The last issue I ran into was that the trash bag kept falling off the trash can and getting pilled under trash. I first tried a larger trash bag and this did help a bit but it would still fall off over time. Eventually I realized the internal trashcan had handles on the side and I could tie the trash bag ties to them and this completely solved the issue. I could even use the smaller trash bags again with no issue.

Since then I have not had any issues and maintaining it has been as easy as taking it out when it fills.
